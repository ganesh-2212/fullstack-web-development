About Siya Momos

Siya Momos started with a simple goal — to bring authentic street-style momos to students, families, and food lovers in Puducherry. What began as a small food stall has grown into a favorite local snack spot.

We focus on

  • Fresh ingredients
  • Hygienic preparation
  • Consistent taste
  • Quick service
  • Affordable prices

Siya Momos

When did Siya Momos first appear in Pondicherry

Siya Momos began in 2023 as a small homemade food stall in Reddiyarpalayam, Puducherry. The founder, Siya, started preparing momos for friends and neighbors using a family recipe that focused on fresh ingredients and authentic flavor.
